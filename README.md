## ☁️ About me

I'm a Computer Science student who enjoys the moment a messy dataset finally starts to make sense. Most of my work sits where **data, machine learning, and language models** meet: cleaning and modeling data in Python, building retrieval pipelines, and checking whether AI outputs actually hold up.

I've worked with graph-based ML, NLP, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, and LLM evaluation, both in academic research and in real projects. I'm especially curious about how AI and data can support better decisions, and how we can tell when a model is right for the right reasons.

Outside the code, organizing events and working in teams taught me how to communicate, coordinate, and lead. I learn best by experimenting, so expect a few half-finished ideas alongside the polished ones ♡
